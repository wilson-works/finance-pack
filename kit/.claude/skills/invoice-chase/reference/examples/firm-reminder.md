# Firm Reminder — Repeat-Late-Payer Example

> **When to use:** `repeat-late` score, or `occasionally-late` past 45 days late — a customer with a pattern. Direct and specific, with a pay-by date and a business consequence, but still professional and never a legal threat.

---

**Subject:** Overdue: Invoice [Invoice #] — [Amount] past due

Hi [Customer Name],

Invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] was due on [Due Date] and is now [Days Late] days past due.

Please arrange payment by [Pay-By Date]. You can pay directly here: [Payment Link].

If there's a problem with the invoice itself, reply to this email and I'll address it right away. Otherwise, we'll need this balance settled before we can schedule any new work.

Regards,
[Owner Name]
[Business Name]
